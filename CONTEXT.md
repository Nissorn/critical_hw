# Critical Homework

Critical Homework helps teachers design and review analytical-thinking assignments while giving learners evidence-based, reversible support. The pilot focuses on ป.5 science and keeps integrity review human-controlled.

## Learning

**Teacher**:
A person who prepares lessons, approves assignments and feedback rules, reviews learner evidence, and decides instructional follow-up.
_Avoid_: instructor account, operator

**Learner**:
A primary or secondary student who attempts assignments, requests hints, receives feedback, and revises work.
_Avoid_: ability label, low/high performer

**Lesson**:
The teacher-approved learning objective and material from which an assignment is designed.
_Avoid_: prompt, source pack

**Assignment**:
A teacher-approved learning task that asks a learner to frame a problem, use evidence, reason, evaluate, and transfer understanding.
_Avoid_: quiz, answer sheet

**Rubric**:
The subject-grounded criteria and observable anchors used to assess an assignment's reasoning evidence.
_Avoid_: generic critical-thinking score

**Attempt**:
A learner's answer at a point in the assignment lifecycle, including the first response before feedback and later revisions.
_Avoid_: final answer

**Process Evidence**:
The learner's first attempt, evidence and assumptions, hint history, explanation, and revision that show how understanding developed.
_Avoid_: plagiarism proof

**Hint**:
A constrained instructional cue that helps a learner continue reasoning without completing the assignment.
_Avoid_: solution, answer reveal

**Feedback**:
A rubric-linked observation of what the learner did, what is missing, and what to try next.
_Avoid_: model verdict

## Instructional decisions

**Action Group**:
A temporary, skill-specific instructional state: Diagnose/Reteach, Guided Practice, or Independent Transfer.
_Avoid_: fixed ability group, ranking

**Transfer Task**:
A new but structurally related task used to check whether a learner can apply reasoning without relying on a memorized response or AI-generated answer.
_Avoid_: repeat question

**Integrity Signal**:
A non-conclusive cue that invites teacher review of process evidence; it never proves authorship or misconduct by itself.
_Avoid_: cheating probability, AI verdict

**Access Mode**:
The learner's practical way of participating, including device type and connectivity conditions.
_Avoid_: readiness score

## Reporting and integration

**Class Insight**:
An aggregate view of learning progress, action-group movement, hint use, and review workload for a teacher's authorized class.
_Avoid_: student surveillance dashboard

**Report**:
A teacher-reviewed summary built from authorized Class Insight data and kept separate from grades, discipline, admission, and opportunity decisions.
_Avoid_: automated judgement

**MCP Integration**:
A provider connection that exposes narrowly scoped, teacher-authorized Class Insight tools to an external AI client for draft documentation.
_Avoid_: unrestricted data connector
